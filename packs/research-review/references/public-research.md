# Public X / web research

Record the question, time window and timezone, search terms, languages and
retrieval limits. Start with neutral searches and look for material contrary
evidence. Stop when support, conditions and major counterevidence are sufficient
for the decision; failure to find a counterexample is a bounded search result.
Do not impose a positive/negative quota or generalize prevalence from search hits.

For each useful source preserve its direct URL, speaker, publication time (or
unknown), retrieval time, method, outcome and a short excerpt. An author's
experience, their explanation of the cause and a Bot's explanation are different
claims. Preserve the distinction through compression. Different prices, regions,
versions, plans or retrieval conditions can explain apparent contradictions.

Count posts only when counts help. Define the cohort before counting; list the
included post IDs, deduplicate reposts/quotes and separate independent reports,
hearsay, exclusions and period-unknown cases. Deduplicate authors separately.
Neither likes nor retrieved post counts establish a population majority.

Use original official sources for product facts and concrete user reports for
experiences. State when only an abstract, excerpt, screenshot or search result
was read. Do not promote an unsuccessful retrieval to a successful read, or call
an earlier failure false merely because a later request succeeded differently.

The reviewer checks source → claim → submitted prose, including appendix prose.
Shortening a quotation must not drop its speaker, uncertainty or causal qualifier.
An unresolved material condition stays visible in the final answer. Keep detailed
evidence separate from a compact answer rather than silently removing qualifications.
